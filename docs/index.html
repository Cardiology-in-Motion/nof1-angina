Angina symptom diary

Designed by cardiologists, for the days between appointments.

An “N-of-1” approach focuses on one person: you. Track your symptoms over time, spot patterns, and share meaningful reports with your healthcare team.

Your symptom diary

Android app, shown with sample data.

How it works

  1. Log Record an episode's severity, duration, location and triggers, or use a quick log on the go.
  2. Review Use charts to see how your symptoms change over time.
  3. Share Create a PDF report and send it to your cardiologist.

Also in the app

  • CCS angina grading Record your CCS angina grade over time.
  • Dundee step test A guided step test built right into the app.
  • Medications Keep a simple record of your current medications, always to hand at appointments.

Your data stays yours

On iPhone, your diary syncs through your own iCloud account. On Android, it is stored on your device. No accounts, no ads, no tracking. You choose when to share your records.

Read the privacy policy

This app is for personal symptom tracking only. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult your healthcare professional.
