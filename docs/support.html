Getting Help

If you experience a technical issue, have a feature request, or need assistance using the app, please email cardiologyinmotion@gmail.com.

You can also open an issue on our GitHub repository. Issues are public, so please don't include any health details:

https://github.com/cardiology-in-motion/nof1-angina/issues

Please include your device model, your iOS or Android version, and the app version (shown in the app's Info screen) when reporting a bug.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I share a report with my healthcare professional?

Go to the Report tab and generate a PDF report. You can then use your phone's share menu to send it via email, AirDrop (iPhone), or another sharing method.

How do I delete a diary entry?

In the Diary tab, swipe left on an entry. On iPhone, tap “Delete”; deletions sync across your devices via iCloud. On Android, the entry is deleted as you swipe.

What happens to my data if I uninstall the app?

App data stored locally on your device will be removed when you uninstall the app. If iCloud sync is enabled, data may remain in your iCloud account until removed from all synced devices or deleted via your iCloud settings. On Android, uninstalling removes all of the app's data from your device; a copy may remain in your Google backup if backup is turned on.

What happens if I change phones?

iPhone: your diary syncs via iCloud, so it appears on your new iPhone when you sign in with the same Apple ID.
Android: keep Google backup turned on so your diary moves to your new phone.
Data does not transfer between iPhone and Android. Before switching, create a PDF report to keep a record of your history.

Privacy

Your data is stored on your device. On iPhone, it is synced securely using Apple’s iCloud services; on Android, it stays on your device. No data is transmitted to third-party servers.

Medical Disclaimer

This app is intended for personal symptom tracking only. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional regarding medical concerns or decisions.
